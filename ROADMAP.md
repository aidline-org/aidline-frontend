# Roadmap

Where the Aidline frontend is heading. GitHub is the source of truth for status: see [open issues](https://github.com/aidline-org/aidline-frontend/issues) and [ISSUES.md](ISSUES.md).

## Now: polish the core flows

- Loading states, skip link and relative times across the app
- Copy and share buttons on campaign pages
- Light and dark theme toggle
- Transaction stepper for every wallet action
- Show refunds in giving history

## Next: trust and discovery

- On chain proof panel on campaign pages
- Verifier profile pages
- Shareable donation receipts
- Open Graph images per campaign
- Upload a cover photo when creating a campaign
- Live chain status in the header

## Quality

- End to end smoke tests with Playwright
- Automated accessibility checks
- French and Spanish translations

## Later

- Map of campaigns on the home page
- Notifications when a campaign you backed releases a milestone
- Partner dashboards for associations that embed the donate widget

## Before mainnet

- Review of every wallet flow against the audited contract
- Performance and accessibility budgets enforced in CI
- Content review of copy that describes risk and refunds

## How to influence this

Open an issue describing the problem you want solved, or comment on an existing one. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
